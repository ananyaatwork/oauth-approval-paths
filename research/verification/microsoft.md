# Microsoft (Entra ID / Graph): verification log

Checked 2026-10-21 by agent against live learn.microsoft.com pages (plus two secondary blogs for dates Microsoft's docs don't give).

| # | Guide's claim | What the live page says | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Both paths list Partner Center CPP verification (3–5 business days) as a prerequisite, including the low-risk User.Read/openid/profile path. | Default policy: "End users can consent for any user consentable delegated permissions EXCEPT" a fixed list. It has no verified-publisher condition ([manage-app-consent-policies](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/entra/identity/enterprise-apps/manage-app-consent-policies)). Step-up applies only to "newly registered multitenant apps that aren't publisher verified and require nonbasic permissions" ([step-up consent](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/entra/identity/enterprise-apps/configure-risk-based-step-up-consent)). It applies to apps "which use OAuth 2.0 to request permissions that extend beyond the basic sign-in and read user profile" ([publisher verification overview](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/entra/identity-platform/publisher-verification-overview)). | CORRECTED. Path 1 is now "Basic sign-in (works unverified)" with setup only, and verification is optional. A new path 2 covers other user-consentable scopes, where verification is needed. Headline and nextAction are rewritten. Caveat: tenants on microsoft-user-default-low or with user consent off still block unverified apps. |
| 2 | Since about Aug 2025 the default policy blocks Files, Sites, Mail, Calendar, Chat, Contacts and Tasks scopes, even for verified apps. | Microsoft's live list includes Files.\*.All, Sites.\*.All, Mail.\*, Calendars.\*, Chat.\*, OnlineMeetings.\*, Contacts.ReadWrite/.Shared, Tasks.\*, People.Read, EAS/EWS/IMAP/POP. It has no verified exception, but the page gives no dates (manage-app-consent-policies). MC1097272 (17 June 2025) covered files and sites only: "begin rolling out in mid-July and are due to be in all tenants sometime in August 2025" ([office365itpros](https://office365itpros.com/2025/06/19/app-consent-policy-user-app-consent/)). Exchange/Teams scopes rolled out "between the end of October and late November 2025" ([AdminDroid](https://blog.admindroid.com/microsoft-requires-admin-consent-for-apps-accessing-exchange-teams-apis/)). | CORRECTED. The scope list matches the live docs. The Aug 2025 date applies only to Files/Sites. Mail, Calendar, Chat and meetings scopes came in late 2025 (secondary source). The date Contacts, Tasks and People were added could not be confirmed and is listed as an unknown. |
| 3 | Partner Center verification usually takes 3–5 business days. | "In most cases, the process takes three to five business days to complete. The note now says "in most cases" and mentions the per-check ETA. |

(step-up consent) | MATCH. This is specific to the step-up case when the admin consent workflow is off. |
| 6 | Publisher verification is free and done "in minutes" once prerequisites are met. | "Developers who have already met these requirements can be verified in minutes. No charges are associated…" (publisher verification overview) | MATCH |

**Summary:** 6 claims checked: 4 MATCH, 2 CORRECTED, 0 COULD NOT VERIFY (one sub-date, Contacts/Tasks/People, is recorded as an unknown). The main fix is structural: publisher verification does not block basic sign-in under the default policy, so the fastest path takes minutes, not 3–5 business days.
