| # | Guide's claim | What the live page says | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1a | Testing mode allows up to 100 test users | "limited to up to 100 test users listed in the OAuth consent screen" ([s1](https://support.google.com/cloud/answer/15549945)) | MATCH |
| 1b | Testing-mode authorizations and refresh tokens expire after 7 days, except openid/email/profile-only apps | "Authorizations by a test user will expire seven days from the time of consent", and the refresh token "will also expire" ([s1](https://support.google.com/cloud/answer/15549945)). "is issued a refresh token expiring in 7 days … unless the only OAuth scopes requested are a subset of name, email address, and user profile" ([s2](https://developers.google.com/identity/protocols/oauth2)) | MATCH |
| 2 | Unverified production apps are capped at 100 new users for the project's lifetime, and the cap can't be reset | The cap is 100 new users and "applies over the entire lifetime of the project"; it "cannot be reset or changed" ([s1](https://support.google.com/cloud/answer/15549945)). The page adds that Google may adjust limits for some apps. | MATCH |
| 3 | Google's pages disagree on sensitive-scope review: FAQ "10 business days" vs developer page "3–5" | FAQ: sensitive scope verification "10 Business days" ([s7](https://support.google.com/cloud/answer/13463817)). Developer page: "typically takes 3-5 business days to complete" ([s9](https://developers.google.com/identity/protocols/oauth2/production-readiness/sensitive-scope-verification)). Both figures are still live. | MATCH |
| 4 | Restricted-scope review is "6 weeks", plus an annual CASA assessment | FAQ: "6 weeks" ([s7](https://support.google.com/cloud/answer/13463817)). Developer page: "can potentially take several weeks", and apps must "complete a security assessment at least every 12 months" ([s10](https://developers.google.com/identity/protocols/oauth2/production-readiness/restricted-scope-verification)). CASA page: "All applications must be revalidated every year" ([s6](https://support.google.com/cloud/answer/13465431)). One gap: s6 says all restricted-scope apps need CASA, but s10 ties it to apps that "store or transmit restricted scope data on servers". | MATCH (added a note on the scope gap) |
| 5 | A manual brand review takes 2–3 business days | FAQ: brand verification "2-3 Business days", with no split between automated and manual ([s7](https://support.google.com/cloud/answer/13463817)). Developer page: brand verification "typically takes 2-3 business days" and "The automated review usually completes in a few minutes"; it gives no time for a manual review ([s10](https://developers.google.com/identity/protocols/oauth2/production-readiness/restricted-scope-verification)). | CORRECTED: the 2–3 day figure now applies to brand verification overall, with no separate time stated for a manual review (timeline note and blocker) |

Extra check: the cap of 100 refresh tokens per Google Account per client, with the oldest token invalidated "without warning", matches [s2](https://developers.google.com/identity/protocols/oauth2).

**Summary:** 6 claims checked: 5 MATCH, 1 CORRECTED, 0 COULD NOT VERIFY. The guide had applied the brand-review figure to manual review only; I also added a note that Google's two pages disagree on which restricted-scope apps need CASA. I used no new sources and changed no confidence levels.
